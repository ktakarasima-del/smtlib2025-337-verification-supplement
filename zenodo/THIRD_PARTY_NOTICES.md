The [SMT-LIB 2025 official benchmark release](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.16740866) supplies the original inputs in `QF_DT.tar.zst` and `QF_IDL.tar.zst`. The summary preserves the official relative paths and the CC BY 4.0 per-benchmark license reported by its catalogue. The source files and their author attributions are available from the official release. This supplement distributes certificate data and verification sources. The official problem files are obtained from the upstream record.

The wire-routing witness includes normalized grid and terminal facts for the Wire Routing instance associated with the [ASP Competition benchmark encodings](https://dtai.cs.kuleuven.be/events/ASP-competition/encodings.shtml). The witness also identifies the official SMT-LIB input by SHA-256 and supplies the implications needed to validate the proof against that input. Upstream encodings and data files remain with their original sources.

The [prior 179-certificate record](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24416131) supplies the 179 previously reported outcomes and their published evidence. The summary identifies these rows by the prior record DOI.
